# Review Request Templates

Personalize each message. Keep it warm and low-pressure. Replace `[link]` with the full `/review/` URL once the domain is live.

## SMS request

> Hi [First name], it's Amie. It was a pleasure helping with your move. If you have a few minutes, I'd be grateful if you'd share what the experience was like. It helps others who are making a similar move. No pressure at all. [link]

## Email request

**Subject:** Would you share your experience?

> Hi [First name],
>
> I hope you're settling in well. Thank you again for trusting me with your move.
>
> If you're open to it, I'd be grateful if you'd share a few honest words about what working together was like. Your perspective helps other people understand what to expect. It only takes a couple of minutes, and you choose how your name is shown.
>
> [link]
>
> Whether or not you have time, thank you. It was a pleasure.
>
> Warmly,
> Amie

## Follow-up (once, a couple of weeks later)

> Hi [First name], a quick, gentle follow-up in case my earlier note got buried. If you'd still like to share your experience, here's the link: [link]. And if now isn't the right time, no worries at all. Thank you either way.

## Notes

- Send once, follow up once, then let it rest.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review.
- Do not suggest what to write.
